Assuming these are natural-language test questions for Saarthi against `lending_demo_db`, graded by the joins and logic each one forces.

**Simple** (single table, basic filter/aggregate)

1. How many active branches are there in each region?
2. Which loan products have a maximum tenure above 60 months?
3. How many customers have KYC status Pending?
4. What is the total disbursed amount across all Active loans?
5. How many collection receipts came through UPI versus NACH?

**Medium** (2-3 joins, grouping, date logic)

1. What is the total disbursed amount and average interest rate per product category?
2. Which 10 branches have the highest number of NPA loans, and what is each one's NPA share of its total loans?
3. What is the application approval rate by product, and what are the top 3 rejection reasons overall?
4. Which agents sourced the most disbursed loans last financial year, and what was their total sanctioned amount?
5. What is the average CIBIL score and average monthly income of customers by occupation, for those with at least one active loan?

**Complicated** (multi-hop joins, window functions, cohorts, reconciliation)

1. For each disbursement-month cohort, what percentage of loans ever crossed 90 DPD within 12 months of disbursement, split by product?
2. What is the monthly collection efficiency (amount collected ÷ EMI due) by region for the last 12 months, and which regions declined three months in a row?
3. As of the latest snapshot date, what is the total provisioning amount by asset classification and product category, and which 5 branches carry the highest provisioning as a percentage of outstanding principal?
4. Which agents have an NPA rate on their sourced loans more than double their branch's average, and how much commission (paid plus pending) is tied to those agents?
5. Which loans show a mismatch between the ledger and the loan record: disbursed amount not equal to the sum of Disbursement transactions, or processing fee not matching the product's fee percentage on the sanctioned amount?

Two of these deliberately test ambiguity handling: "last financial year" (April-March) and "collection efficiency" (needs a defined formula). Medium 4 and Complicated 4 also test whether the path from loans to agents is found via `loan_applications`, since `loans` has no `agent_id`.
