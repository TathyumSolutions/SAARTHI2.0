Manufacturing Schema

The physical data model behind Saarthi's synthetic engineering / auto-components manufacturer demo database (demo_manufacturing/db_manufacturing.py): 16 tables covering plant and machine master data, the product catalogue and bill of materials, raw-material procurement, production orders and their per-shift shop-floor log, quality inspections, machine downtime, shipments, payables and the sales/receipt ledger. All data is synthetic (Faker en_IN locale; GSTIN/PAN/mobile numbers are valid-format, not real registrants) and is provisioned into its own Postgres database, manufacturing_demo_db, independent of Saarthi's own core databases and of lending_demo_db.
